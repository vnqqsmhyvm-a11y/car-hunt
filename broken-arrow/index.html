Broken Arrow dealer row

Route checked Fri Oct 16, 2026 (about 9:05–9:20 AM CT; Kia Sportages added about 2:15 PM CT)

Five dealers within about 3 miles of each other on Elm Pl, Aspen Ave and Queens St in Broken Arrow, about 20 minutes from home via the BA Expressway (Hwy 51). Allow 2½–3 hours.

Suggested order: Ferguson (CX-5 and four Kia Sportages) → DrivePoint Autos (Sportage, 1522 N Elm, across from Ferguson) → Quality Value (south on Elm) → Nelson Nissan (Queens St) → Chris Nikel (Aspen Ave, CX-30 and Mazda3s). Next stop after this: South Tulsa, about 15–20 minutes.

Call ahead to make sure the car is still there. Asking prices shown; Cars.com prices usually exclude the dealer doc fee ($300–$700).

At each stop, ask for:

  • Carfax (or AutoCheck) for the exact VIN
  • Rental or fleet history: skip it if it was a rental
  • Out-the-door price including the doc fee
  • Open recalls done (run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls)
