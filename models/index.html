  • Hyundai/Kia 1.6-turbo dual-clutch trimsAny year

    Avoid

    The 7-speed dual-clutch has needed service campaigns for shift shock and a snap-ring fix (Hyundai T7E) and shift-logic updates (Kia SA454). The 2.0 with the IVT is the simpler, safer choice for a new driver.

    Applies to Elantra N Line, Kona 1.6T (through 2023), Forte GT and Seltos 1.6T. Ratings vary by model; see the matching entries above.

  • Hyundai/Kia key-start cars without an immobilizer2016–2021

    Avoid

    Thieves can start them with a USB cable, and some insurers limited new policies on these cars in 2023. Skip any key-start Hyundai/Kia unless a dealer record shows the anti-theft software is done; push-button start is not affected.

    Roughly 8.3 million 2011–2022 key-start Hyundai/Kia cars were affected (AP). Ratings vary by model; see the matching entries above.
